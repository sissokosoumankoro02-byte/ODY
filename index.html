04 — ODYSSEY

ODŸ is about where you come from, what you carry with you and where you decide to go next.

ODŸ is a streetwear project built around graphic identity, personal references and everyday movement.

Rooted in Vitry, the brand combines vintage imagery, urban culture, custom details and technical influences. ODŸ moves between relaxed, fitted and functional silhouettes without being limited to one style.

The name comes from “Odyssey”, a reference to movement, direction and the path ahead. The Ÿ symbol reflects the choices that shape that path, while the GPS coordinates mark the project's point of origin.
